This Privacy Policy explains how the AI-Based Visual Risk and Compliance Intelligence System, referred to as "Visual Risk and AI Intelligence", "the application", "we", or "our", may collect, use, store, and disclose information when you use the platform.

The platform is designed to help users review personal goals, financial activity, study performance, fitness activity, and predictive insights. The exact information processed depends on the features you use and the application's actual implementation.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features implemented, the application may process:

  • Account information: Name, email address, username, and authentication-related information.
  • Financial information: Income, expenses, savings, budgets, spending categories, and financial goals entered by you.
  • Academic information: GPA, grades, study sessions, study duration, productivity records, and academic goals.
  • Fitness information: Exercise activity, workout duration, estimated calories, fitness goals, and activity streaks.
  • Goals and preferences: Personal objectives, progress records, planning preferences, and information you voluntarily provide.
  • AI conversations: Questions, prompts, and other information you submit to an AI assistant, if that feature is enabled.
  • Technical information: Limited session, browser, application, and diagnostic information where collected by the deployed application or its infrastructure.

We should collect only the information needed for the stated features. Avoid entering passwords, bank login credentials, payment card details, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information unless a feature genuinely requires it and appropriate protections are implemented.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts.
  • Display dashboards, summaries, charts, and activity histories.
  • Calculate financial summaries and spending trends.
  • Review study patterns and academic progress.
  • Track fitness activity and progress toward goals.
  • Generate forecasts, simulations, and predictive insights.
  • Respond to questions submitted through the AI assistant.
  • Maintain application functionality and investigate technical issues.
  • Protect the application against unauthorized access and misuse.

Information should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or, where required, your consent.

3. Artificial Intelligence and Predictions

Visual Risk and AI Intelligence may use machine learning models, statistical calculations, or third-party AI services to provide forecasts, recommendations, conversational responses, and what-if simulations.

  • Predictions are estimates based on available inputs and model assumptions. They are not guarantees of future outcomes.
  • AI-generated responses may be incomplete, inaccurate, or unsuitable for your individual circumstances.
  • Financial projections are for informational and planning purposes and are not professional financial, investment, or tax advice.
  • Academic and fitness predictions should not be treated as definitive judgments about your ability, health, or future performance.
  • If a third-party AI provider is integrated, information submitted to that provider may be processed under its applicable terms and privacy policy.

If the Gemini API or another external AI service is enabled, disclose which information is transmitted, why it is transmitted, and which provider processes it. Do not describe AI processing as local or private unless the actual implementation supports that claim.

AI outputs should support your decision-making, not replace your own judgment or qualified professional advice.

4. Information Sharing

Personal information should not be sold. Information may be disclosed only when appropriate and necessary, such as:

  • Service providers: Hosting, database, analytics, or AI providers required to operate enabled features.
  • Legal obligations: Where disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal process.
  • Security: Where reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect users, or safeguard the application.
  • With your direction: When you explicitly request or authorize a particular disclosure.

The application should not expose one user's private financial, academic, fitness, or account information to another user. Access controls must be implemented and tested to enforce this separation.

5. Data Storage and Security

The application may use a database such as SQLite to store account records and application data. The actual storage location and hosting environment depend on the deployment configuration.

Reasonable safeguards should be implemented to protect information, including appropriate authentication, access controls, secure secret management, input validation, and HTTPS in production.

No method of storing or transmitting data can be guaranteed completely secure. The security of this application depends on its implementation, configuration, hosting provider, and operational practices.

API keys, database credentials, and Flask secret keys must not be published in source code or exposed in the browser.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as needed to provide the relevant features, maintain legitimate records, or meet applicable legal obligations.

Before deployment, document how users can request account deletion and how associated records, backups, and information held by external providers are handled.

Deleting an account in the interface should not be described as deleting all copies of its information unless the application and its providers actually perform those operations.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the laws applicable to you, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information held about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Ask questions about data collection and third-party processing.

To exercise an applicable right, contact the project administrator using the contact details provided below. Requests may be subject to reasonable identity verification and legal limitations.

8. Cookies and Sessions

A Flask-based deployment may use session cookies to maintain authentication and preserve application state. Other cookies or similar technologies should be disclosed if they are introduced.

Session cookies and authentication settings should be configured securely in production. Disabling cookies may prevent certain application features from working correctly.

9. Children's Privacy

The application is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe that a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact the application administrator so the matter can be reviewed.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised when the application's features, data practices, service providers, or legal obligations change. The updated version should display its revision date.

Where required, users will be notified of material changes and asked for consent when appropriate.

11. Contact Information

For privacy questions, data access requests, or deletion requests, contact the person responsible for administering this application.
